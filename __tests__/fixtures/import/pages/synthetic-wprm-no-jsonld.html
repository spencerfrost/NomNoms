Saucy Meatballs

Ingredients

Meatballs

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • ½ cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 cloves garlic minced

Sauce

  • 1 (28 ounce) can crushed tomatoes
  • 2-3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil

Instructions

Meatballs

  • Mix the beef, breadcrumbs, egg and garlic.
  • Roll into 16 balls.

Sauce

  • Brown the meatballs in the oil.
  • Add the tomatoes and basil.
  • Simmer for 20 minutes.

Notes

Freeze leftovers for up to 3 months.
