Easy Beef Stew

A weeknight beef stew.

Serves 6 · Prep 20 min · Cook 2 hours

Ingredients

  • 2 lb. beef chuck, cubed
  • 1 ½ cups beef broth
  • 3 medium carrots, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • Salt &amp; pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Brown the beef in batches in a heavy pot.
  2. Add the tomato paste and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Pour in the broth, add the carrots and bring to a simmer.
  4. Cover and cook on low for 2 hours, until the beef is tender.
