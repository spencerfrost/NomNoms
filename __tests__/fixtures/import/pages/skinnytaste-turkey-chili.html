Turkey Chili Stuffed Acorn Squash

These Stuffed Acorn Squash are the coziest fall dinner and turned out even better than I imagined. The turkey chili filling is mild, savory, and so comforting, pairing perfectly with the natural sweetness of the roasted acorn squash. It feels wholesome and hearty yet light enough for a weeknight. If you love this recipe, don’t miss my Farro and Sausage Stuffed Acorn Squash.

Why This Works

I had this stuffed squash for dinner last night and loved every bite. It was so filling and delicious that I’m excited to have leftovers for lunch today. It reheats really well. If you’re saving half for another night, just leave the cheese off until you’re ready to reheat. Here’s more reasons why you’ll love this dish!

  • It’s high in protein and fiber, keeping you full and satisfied.
  • A fun, seasonal twist that feels comforting yet light.
  • Great for meal prep because both the chili and the squash reheat beautifully.
  • Easy to adapt for dairy-free or Whole30 by simply skipping the cheese.

Ingredients You’ll Need

With this healthy stuffed acorn squash recipe, I love that you get an edible bowl that’s good for you and tastes great! Roasted squash and chili make the best combo! See the recipe card below for exact measurements.

  • Acorn Squash: You don’t have to peel acorn squash. Just cut them in half and scrape out the seeds. If you’re having a hard time cutting them, microwave them for a couple of minutes.
  • 93% lean ground turkey provides protein with little fat.
  • Seasoning: Kosher salt, black pepper, cumin, chili powder, paprika
  • Aromatics: Chopped onion and crushed garlic
  • Tomatoes: Canned Rotel mild tomatoes with green chilies and tomato sauce
  • Water thins the chili.
  • Herbs: Simmer a bay leaf in the chili, then garnish with fresh cilantro at the end.
  • Cheese: Top each squash with shredded sharp cheddar cheese.

How to Make Stuffed Acorn Squash

If the chili doesn’t seem thick enough after simmering, continue simmering it uncovered for about 5 minutes to reduce the liquid. See the recipe card at the bottom for printable directions.

  1. Roast the acorn squash: Place the squash halves, cut side down, on an oiled sheet pan. Bake at 400°F for 30 to 35 minutes.
  2. Turkey and aromatics: Brown the ground turkey in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Season with salt and pepper. Then reduce the heat to medium and add the onions and garlic.
  3. Simmer the chili: Add the remaining chili ingredients (Rotel tomatoes, tomato sauce, water, the bay leaf, and all the spices). Reduce the heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer for 25 minutes.
  4. Stuff the squash: Fill each half with chili, then top with cheese. Bake for 5 minutes, until the cheese melts, then sprinkle with cilantro.

Variations

  • Meat: Swap ground turkey for lean ground chicken or beef.
  • Squash: Substitute butternut squash, spaghetti squash, or delicata squash. (You’ll need to adjust the bake times. Click the links for instructions.)
  • Don’t have Rotel? Use canned diced tomatoes and green chilies instead.
  • Make it spicy: Add chopped jalapeños or serranos while sautéing the onions, or use regular or spicy Rotel tomatoes.
  • Add more protein and fiber: Stir in a can of kidney, pinto, or black beans.
  • If you need a dairy-free or Whole30-friendly option, omit the cheese.
  • Don’t like cilantro? Leave it off.
  • Add more toppings, like avocado, low-fat sour cream, or plain Greek yogurt.

Storage and Meal Prep Tips

  • Meal prep: Cut and clean the squash halves and make the chili a day early. The next night, before dinner, roast the squash and reheat the chili over low heat on the stove.
  • Refrigerate the acorn squash and chili in separate containers, or with the chili in the squash halves, for up to 4 days. If I know I’m going to have leftovers, I leave off the cheese and add it before reheating.
  • To reheat the stuffed squash, sprinkle with cheddar and bake at 350°F for 20 to 25 minutes, or until heated through. If you’re taking leftovers to work, microwave for 1 to 2 minutes until warm.
  • Freeze: While I wouldn’t recommend freezing the acorn squash, you can freeze the chili for up to 3 months and cook fresh squash before serving. I like using Souper Cubes because I can pop out one cube for each squash.

Turkey Chili Stuffed Acorn Squash

Yield: 4 servings
Serving Size: 1 stuffed half

Ingredients

  • 2 acorn squash, halved, seeded
  • 1 pound 93% lean ground turkey
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • ⅓ cup onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 10 ounces canned Rotel mild tomatoes with green chilies
  • ½ cup canned tomato sauce
  • ½ cup water
  • ¾ tsp cumin
  • ¼ tsp chili powder
  • ¼ tsp paprika
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 6 tablespoons shredded sharp cheese, omit for Whole 30 and dairy free
  • fresh cilantro, for garnish

Instructions

  • Preheat oven to 400°F degrees. Spray a baking sheet with nonstick spray.
  • Place squash halves on the baking sheet, cut sides down. Bake until soft, 30 to 35 minutes.
  • Meanwhile, in a large skillet, brown turkey over medium-high heat, breaking it up as it cooks into smaller pieces and season with salt and pepper.
  • When meat is browned and cooked through add onion and garlic; cook 3 minutes over medium heat.
  • Add the can of Rotel tomatoes, tomato sauce, water, cumin, chili powder, paprika, and bay leaf.
  • Cover and simmer over medium-low heat about 25 minutes stirring occasionally.
  • Remove bay leaf, flip the squash over and fill each half with ¾ cup chili.
  • Top with cheese and bake until melted, about 5 minutes. Top with cilantro.
